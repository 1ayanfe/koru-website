About KORU Health

Getting to know our website and what it stands for.

Who we are

KORU Health is a simple health and wellness information platform created as part of an HTML, CSS and JavaScript project. The aim is to make general health topics easier to understand.

Our mission

Our mission is to encourage people to learn about healthy habits and make informed choices in their daily lives.

Our vision

Our vision is a community where people have access to clear and useful health education.
